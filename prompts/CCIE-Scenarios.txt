Model: Sonnet 5.5 Medium

I am preparing for my CCIE Enterprise core, and have completed a skills assessment to evaluate where I stand on the CCIE Enterprise Infrastructure Blueprint.

Using the knowledge assessment, create CCIE level design scenarios to help assess my skills.

The scenario should outline:

- Customer
- Business Requirements
- Constraints
- Tasks

Provide notes on habits to practice before the questions on tasks.  

For each scenario ask 10 - 15 questions, including single/multiple choice or order of operations.  Provide the scenario in a document, create a simlet environment to run through the questions, once complete provide a result that can be shared for evaluation and review.
